# Medicus Suite — Feature List

**Version:** v4.0.0
**Generated:** 2026-10-16

v4.0.0 is the next release after 3.269.6. It is workflow and display on the Medicus session. It does not make a clinical judgement, recommendation or calculation. A quiet screen is not a completed check. This note is not a sign-off.

## What it is

Medicus Suite is a Chrome extension that sits alongside the Medicus electronic patient record. It adds a side panel, a handful of full-tab tools, and small additions on Medicus's own screens. What it shows is either a count or a list Medicus already holds, or practice-owned text such as a template or a rota. It does not score a patient, recommend a route, interpret a result, or file on the strength of a suite verdict.

## What remains

- **Task presence** — a notice when a colleague already has the open request, from Medicus's own presence channel. Advisory only. Absence of the notice is not evidence that nobody is on it.
- **Templates and document organiser** — groups Medicus's own template and document lists and opens Medicus's own form. The suite does not post the record.
- **Rota** — duty cover, leave, and the full Rota Manager tab. Safe-staffing lines are practice settings. They warn. They do not block.
- **Slot Counter and Capacity Forecast** — taken and free counts, and the practice's own minimums. An incomplete scan is not "no days at risk".
- **Submissions, Activity, Referrals** — counts and charts of work already in Medicus.
- **Waiting room and request strips** — how long someone has waited, and how many requests are in. Thresholds are operational.
- **Breach strip** — Medicus's own urgency flag and the clock. It is not a suite clinical score.
- **Workload tracker** — open-task counts on the workflow dashboard. A dash is not zero. Read-only.
- **Availability wall** — unbooked slots on a triage-room display. No patient names. No write.
- **Duplicate checker and Contacts** — problem and contact tidy tools. They do not score clinical risk.
- **Patient alerts** — text the practice wrote for that patient, shown when the record is open.
- **Phrases, Knowledge, Leaflets** — practice text and NHS leaflet links. Copy or open. No patient is scored.
- **Reception capture** — the pathway question list and the booking stop on an unanswered or positive flag remain. A suggested route is not shown.
- **Triage chips** — on each triage request row: age, priority, how long the task has waited, and keyword chips (for example chest pain or a mental-health crisis) plus Pharmacy First or ask-back when the request text matches a pathway. Absence of a keyword chip is not proof the contact is safe.
- **Signing queue** — the list of prescription requests Medicus already holds.
- **Health strip** — the suite checking its own page contract. It is not a patient score.

## Settings

Options holds practice code, tab choice, notifications, slots, capacity, submissions, task presence, triage chip rules, leaflets, backup, and the operational packs (allocation canvases, contacts, workload, availability wall).
